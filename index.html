02 / About Mila

At 24, I was made redundant. At 27, I became Chief of Staff.

Mila Milićević · London
Fractional COO · Operating Partner
Resident Operations Adviser, Founders House

Three years earlier, I'd lost my job and had to start again.

By 27, I was working alongside founders and senior leadership teams, helping them make decisions and run their businesses.

I went on to become a COO, working across financial technology, commercial operations and growing businesses.

Different companies. Different people. But I kept seeing the same problem.

Founders hired people to take work off their plates, then found themselves managing those people, answering their questions and making their decisions.

They'd delegated the work. They hadn't delegated the responsibility.

And often, it wasn't because they didn't want to.

Nobody had put the structure in place for anyone else to take it.

That's what I do now. I build the part of the business that allows the founder to step back.

Concentric · Founded by Mila Milićević

For the decisions you can't take to colleagues.

Concentric is a research-led peer advisory model for professionally established women facing consequential decisions.

Councils of six meet online for 90 minutes each month: the same women each time, with a facilitator who keeps the discussion on the decision. Each member starts with Keel, a short profile of how she weighs evidence and commits.
