What Keel shows you.

Five minutes, a written report, and a one to one debrief. Keel is our own instrument, built for reflection. It is not a clinical or validated test.

  1. 01How you thinkWhether you test claims or trust them, and whether you solve problems through structure or pattern.
  2. 02How you decideHow fast you commit and how long you keep options open, placed on two axes: method and pace.
  3. 03Whether your confidence is earnedSix reasoning questions, each with how sure you are. The gap between confidence and accuracy is the costliest blind spot in a senior role.
  4. 04What it means for your decision nowYour report applies it to the decision you are weighing, with a 90 day plan you agree in your debrief.

Is Concentric for you?

It is, if

  • You are senior, and the decision in front of you is a consequential one.
  • You want challenge from women outside your company and your circle.
  • You can commit 90 minutes a month, and you will bring something real.

It isn’t, if

  • You want coaching, therapy or a networking group. Concentric is none of these.
  • You want someone to tell you what to do. Your decisions stay yours.
  • You are looking for introductions or a bigger network.

Meet the format before you commit to a Council.

Your first session is in an open Council, so you meet the format and the women before you are placed.

If it isn’t right for you, cancel before your second month and pay nothing more.

Who are the other women?+

Women at a similar stage from different sectors, placed in your Council around experience, current challenges and complementary perspectives.

Is it confidential?+

Yes. Every member agrees to keep what is said in the Council confidential before her first session.

What if I can’t make a session?+

Tell your facilitator in advance and join the next open Council that month.

How is this different from coaching?+

Nobody coaches. A facilitator keeps the conversation on the decision, and five peers bring their own experience. You decide what to do with it.

Can I cancel?+

Yes. Membership renews automatically until you cancel, and you can cancel at any time.
