Keel

What Keel shows you.

01

How you think

Whether you test claims or trust them, and whether you solve problems through structure or pattern.

02

How you decide

How fast you commit, and how long you keep options open. Your profile places you on two axes: method and pace.

03

Whether your confidence is earned

Six reasoning questions, each with how sure you are. The gap between confidence and accuracy is the costliest blind spot in a senior role.

04

What it means for your decision now

Your report applies all of it to the decision you are weighing, in your own words, with a 90 day plan you agree in your debrief.

Keel is our own instrument, built for reflection and coaching. It is not a clinical or validated test.
