The Founding criteria

There is no title requirement.

What matters is the decision you are carrying and your commitment to the room.

Concentric is for women who

  • are leading, building or moving toward a senior role, in any field, employed or running their own business
  • have a decision in play that matters to their work or career
  • will be there every month, because a Council only works when everyone comes
  • will keep what is said in the room inside the room
  • are based in the US, as Founding Councils meet on US time

It is not for

  • finding clients, selling or recruiting
  • coaching or advice on demand
  • a single conversation

How it works

  1. Apply below. It takes about two minutes.
  2. We check your answers against the criteria straight away.
  3. If you meet them, you can join on the spot. A few applications need a closer look, and we reply to those within one working day.
