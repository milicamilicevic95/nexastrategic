About Concentric

A better room for decisions still in play.

Concentric runs small, facilitated Councils for women in leadership, built around Keel, our decision profile. It began with one question: why do women with extensive networks still lack the right people to discuss important decisions with?

67

professionally established women in the United States

The research

What 67 Women Told Us.

Our first study gathered responses about professional challenges, existing support and unmet needs, from women navigating significant career moments.

Our approach

How we build the room.

Keel first

Every member starts with her decision profile, so the Council works on how she decides as well as what.

Small groups

Six women per Council, the same each month.

Careful placement

Members placed around professional experience, current challenges and complementary perspectives.

Independent decisions

Members share advice and experience. Each woman decides what to do with it.

Neutral facilitation

A facilitator keeps the conversation on the decision. Nobody coaches.
