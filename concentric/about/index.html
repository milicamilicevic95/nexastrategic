ABOUT CONCENTRIC

A better room for decisions still in play.

Concentric runs small, facilitated Councils for senior women, built around Keel, our decision profile.

It began with one question: why do women with extensive networks still lack the right people to discuss important decisions with? We asked 67 women in the United States. Read what they told us.

OUR APPROACH

How we build the room.

Keel first

Every member starts with her decision profile, so the Council works on how she decides as well as what.

Small groups

Six women per Council, the same each month.

Careful placement

Members selected around professional experience, current challenges and complementary perspectives.

Independent decisions

Members share advice and experience. Each woman decides what to do with it.

Neutral facilitation

A facilitator keeps the conversation on the decision. Nobody coaches.
