THE COUNCIL

Five experienced women in your corner.

Six senior women from different sectors, the same each month. Ninety minutes online, with a facilitator who keeps it on the decision. Every member starts with her Keel report, so the room works on how you decide as well as what.

6 women · 90 minutes · Online

See membership →

WHAT CAN YOU BRING?

Real decisions. Real challenges.

Members might ask what matters most to you, share experiences of similar decisions and help you consider the trade-offs.

You don’t need a major crisis or a fully formed question.

HOW A COUNCIL WORKS

A simple structure, every month.

A member notes the opportunity she is considering and what concerns her.

Your decisions remain yours.

Peer advisory Councils for senior women, built around Keel.
