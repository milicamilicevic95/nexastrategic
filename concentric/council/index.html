The Council

Five experienced women in your corner.

Six senior women from different sectors, the same each month. Ninety minutes online, with a facilitator who keeps it on the decision. Every member starts with her Keel report, so the room works on how you decide as well as what.

Your
decision

What can you bring?

Real decisions. Real challenges.

Should I accept this role? How do I handle a difficult leadership situation? Is it time to change direction? What am I overlooking in my business? How do I approach my next professional move?

Members might ask what matters most to you, share experience of similar decisions and help you weigh the trade-offs. You don’t need a crisis or a fully formed question.

How a Council works

A simple structure, every month.

01

Prepare

Bring one decision, and the question your Keel report surfaced.

02

Discuss

Meet your Council. Ask questions, share experience and look at it from other sides.

03

Reflect

Leave with your next step. Retake Keel at six months.

Your decisions remain yours.
