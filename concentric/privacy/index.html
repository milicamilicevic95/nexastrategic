Who we are

Concentric is created and operated by NexaStrategic Ltd, which decides how your personal information is used and is responsible for it. NexaStrategic Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales, company number 15536373, with its registered office at 7 Bell Yard, London WC2A 2JR, United Kingdom. We are registered with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office under registration number ZB817106.

For anything about your personal information, email concentric@nexastrategic.com. NexaStrategic’s consulting work has its own privacy policy.

What we collect

When you apply. Your name, email, US state, role, LinkedIn profile link, the decision you are facing, how you heard about Concentric, the name of anyone who referred you, any referral code in the link you used, and your confirmation that you can attend monthly and keep the Council confidential. We record the result of our check against the Founding criteria and any notes from a person who reviews your application. We may look at your public LinkedIn profile to review your application.

When you join. Payments are taken by Stripe. We receive your name, email, billing details and payment status. We never see or store your full card number.

When you take Keel. Your answers to the scenario, self-rating and reasoning questions, what you write about the decision or challenge on your mind, your written report and your facilitator’s debrief notes. At six months, your retake answers and progress report.

In your Council. Which Council you are placed in and your attendance. Your facilitator may keep brief notes to prepare sessions and follow up with you. We do not record Council sessions without asking every member first.

When you subscribe or write to us. Your email address and newsletter preferences, and anything you send us by email.

Automatically. Limited technical information our website host needs to run and secure the site, such as your IP address and browser type. The fonts on our pages are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Sensitive information

We do not ask about your health, race, religion, sexuality or other special categories of information. Please leave them out of your application and Keel answers unless they matter to you. If you choose to include them, we use them only to support you in Concentric, we rely on your explicit consent, and you can ask us to remove them at any time.

What you say in Council is shared with the other members of your Council and your facilitator. Every member agrees to keep what is said in the room inside the room.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We share it only with the providers that help us run Concentric, under contract, with our professional advisers such as our accountant, lawyers and insurers, and where the law requires.

International transfers

We are in the UK, and several of our providers store or access information in the United States. Where they do, we rely on UK adequacy regulations, the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum. Email us for details of the safeguard for a particular provider.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to move it to another provider. You can withdraw consent at any time, and unsubscribe from any email. You can ask a person to review any automatic decision about your application. These rights apply wherever you live, including in every US state.

Email concentric@nexastrategic.com. We will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk, or to the privacy regulator or attorney general where you live, though we would like the chance to put it right first.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our pages use your browser’s local storage for two things only: to remember a referral code if you arrived through a referral link, so it can be added to your application, and to save your progress through Keel so you do not lose your answers. Both stay on your device, and you can clear them in your browser settings at any time.

Security, age and changes

We use access controls, two-factor authentication where available, and providers with recognized security standards. No online system is completely secure.

Concentric is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

We will update this notice when Concentric or our providers change, and tell members by email about any material change.
