Legal & privacy.

Information about Concentric, privacy, and how application and Keel data is handled.

Concentric

Concentric is an independent initiative created and operated by NexaStrategic LTD.

Membership terms

Membership is sold by NexaStrategic LTD. It includes Keel and your written report, a thirty minute one to one debrief with your facilitator, a monthly 90 minute Council held online, and a Keel retake with a progress report at six months.

Billing. You pay monthly or annually in advance, by card, through Stripe. Your membership renews automatically at the end of each period, at the same rate, until you cancel. We email annual members at least 30 days before each renewal.

Founding rate. Members who join before January 1, 2027 pay $360 a month or $3,600 a year. That rate stays the same for as long as your membership continues without a break.

Cancelling. You can cancel at any time by emailing concentric@nexastrategic.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for, and you keep your place until then. We do not refund part periods, except as set out below.

Refunds. If you cancel within 14 days of first joining, and before your first Council, we refund you in full. If we cannot place you in a Council within 60 days of joining, you may cancel and we refund you in full. If you join without meeting the published Founding criteria, we refund you in full.

Your Council. We place you with five other women at a similar stage, balancing sector, and avoid direct conflicts of interest. What is said in Council stays there: members agree not to share another member's situation outside the room. Council discussion is peer support, not legal, financial, medical or other professional advice.

Changes. If we change these terms, we will tell you by email at least 30 days before the change applies to you.

These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales. Nothing in them affects rights you have under the law where you live.

Applications

Information you submit through the application form, including your LinkedIn profile, is used to review your application against our published criteria and to contact you about Concentric. Applying does not enroll you or charge you. Your answers are checked automatically against the published Founding criteria; if they meet them, you are offered the option to join straight away, and some applications are reviewed by a person within one working day.

Keel

Keel is available to Concentric members only. Your answers are used to write your personal report and prepare your debrief. They are seen by you and your facilitator.

Your report is drafted with the help of an AI model from your answers, then read, edited and approved by your facilitator before you receive it. Your answers are stored in Airtable, our database provider. Answers may also be used anonymously and in aggregate to improve the program.

Keel is our own instrument, built for reflection and coaching. It is not a clinical, diagnostic or validated psychometric test, and it should not be used for hiring or employment decisions. You can ask us to delete your answers and report at any time.

Research

Research findings published by Concentric are presented with their methodology and limitations. The first US study was directional and based on a self-selected sample.

For legal or privacy enquiries, or to have your data deleted, email concentric@nexastrategic.com.
