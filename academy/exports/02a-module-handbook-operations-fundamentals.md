**Aim**

Teach founders what operations is, from zero, and how its parts connect. By the end they know what each part of operations involves, what "good enough" looks like at their size, and who should own it.

The course answers one question: **what is operations, and who holds it in my business?** It is the first course in the programme. Course 2 (working title: Not your own bottleneck) builds on it.

**Audience**

Founders and owner-managers of early-stage and growing businesses, from just the founder up to about 50 people, with no operations background. Intelligent, busy, expert in their own field. Nothing is assumed: every term is explained the first time it is used.

## OF101 What operations is

**Principle**

Operations is one whole. Every decision touches every panel, so someone has to see the whole ball.

**Method**

Before a decision of any size, turn the ball: look at it from each of the eight panels. Make sure every panel has an owner, and someone holds the whole.

**Tactics**

1. Map the eight panels: who owns each, where it lives, and whether it's owned, only you, or unsure.
2. For any decision that matters, ask the eight "turn the ball" questions before you say yes.
3. Name who, apart from you, sees the whole ball. If nobody does, that's your first gap.

**The eight "turn the ball" questions**

| Panel | Ask |
|---|---|
| Process | What changes in how the work gets done? |
| People | Who does it, and do they have the time and skills? |
| Commercial | Do the price and the terms work for us? |
| Finance and revenue | When does cash go out, and when does it come in? |
| Legal | What are we agreeing to, and who owns what? |
| Compliance | Which rules apply, and could we show we follow them? |
| Governance | Who is allowed to decide this, and who needs to know? |
| Risk | What could go wrong, and could we live with it? |

**Coaching questions**

- Have you thought about which panel you only ever see from one side?
- Consider who, apart from you, sees the whole ball.
- Have you thought about a recent decision you saw from only one panel?

**Learning outcomes**

- OF101.1: Name the eight panels of operations in their four pairs, and say in a sentence what each covers.
- OF101.2: Trace one real decision across all eight panels, and say who in their business sees the whole.
