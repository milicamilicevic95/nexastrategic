Here’s what a thirty-five thousand pound hire really costs, in the UK this tax year. The salary. Employer National Insurance: fifteen percent on everything above five thousand pounds, so four and a half thousand. The minimum employer pension: three percent of qualifying earnings, about eight hundred and sixty. That’s just over forty thousand a year, before equipment, software and your own time getting them started. The Employment Allowance can take up to ten and a half thousand off the business’s total National Insurance bill, though not if your only employee is a director. Then the line at the bottom: with 5.6 weeks of paid holiday, you pay for fifty-two weeks and get about forty-six weeks of work. That isn’t a reason not to hire. It’s a reason to plan for it.
