When you have more than one gap, put them in order. Bottleneck first: fill the gap that costs you the most, in money, missed work, or your time. Doers before managers. A manager needs work and people to manage, and a first manager with nobody to manage ends up doing the work anyway, at a manager’s salary. Titles last. Then the line at the bottom: your next hire is the gap, not the org chart you’d like to have in two years.
