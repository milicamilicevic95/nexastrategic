When founders draw an org chart, they usually start with boxes and titles. Head of this, manager of that. Then they try to fit people in. It feels like progress, but it skips the only question that matters: what work does the business need done? The order on this slide is the whole method. Map the work first. Then put one owner on each piece. Name the role last, after the work it owns.
