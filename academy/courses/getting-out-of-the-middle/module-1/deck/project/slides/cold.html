First, a question. Who’s your next hire? Answer it in your head. [Pause.] Now notice how you answered. Most founders give me a title: an ops manager, a head of sales, a marketing person. A title is a guess at a solution. By the end of this module you’ll have a different kind of answer: the gap in your business, the kind of hire that fills it, and when you can afford it.
