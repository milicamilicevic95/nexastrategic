These are the signs it’s time to hire, on the slide. You’re turning down good work because there’s no capacity. Deadlines or quality are slipping. The same work keeps landing back on your desk. The fourth matters most: you can name the work, and you can count the hours. Vague overwhelm isn’t a job description. If you can’t say what the person would do and how much of it there is, you’re not ready to hire yet. Then, as the line at the bottom says, test it against a slow month. We’ll come to that.
