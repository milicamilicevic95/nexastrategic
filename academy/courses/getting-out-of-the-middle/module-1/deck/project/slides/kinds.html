There are five ways to fill a gap, and only two of them are an employee. Full-time: core, ongoing work you direct, and the biggest commitment. Part-time: core work that doesn’t fill a week. They have the same rights as full-timers, pro rata, including holiday. Contractor or freelancer: a deliverable, a busy period, or a specialist skill. The watch-out, on the right, is status, which we’ll come to. Fractional or agency: senior judgement for a few days a month. You’re buying experience, not hours. At the bottom, software or a better process. Sometimes the honest answer to who’s your next hire is nobody.
