How I review it

Six questions, answered by me

  1. Are all eight parts there?
  2. Could it only be this business? Specific to Kestrel or to you, not generic.
  3. Is now kept apart from next?
  4. Does every part have a name? One person, not a team.
  5. Did the decision move, with a limit? Part B handover: what they decide alone, up to what.
  6. Could a specialist act on the brief? Part A brief: same day, without a call first.

Each is a yes or a not yet. You get back Ready, or Not yet with the one thing to redo. No score, no certificate, and no AI marks anything.
