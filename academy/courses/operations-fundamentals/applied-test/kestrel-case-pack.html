The opportunity

Client C wants Manchester

Client C, the year-long contract from module 1, wants Kestrel to run its events in Manchester too: 12 events from April 2027 to March 2028, worth about £620,000 in fees, on the same 60-day terms. Manchester venues want a 30% deposit at booking, so April’s events need deposits paid in January and February. Two smaller Manchester prospects, worth about £150,000 between them, have asked for proposals but haven’t signed.

Running Manchester needs a local events lead and two coordinators (hired or freelance), Manchester venues and suppliers, and travel between the two cities. Kestrel has no office there.

The founder’s view

“We should say yes this week, before they ask someone else. Marcus can run it from London for the first few months, and we’ll hire once it’s proven.”
