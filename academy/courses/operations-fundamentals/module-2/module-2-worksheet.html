Operations Fundamentals · Module 2

Out of your head.

About 45 minutes, then the stranger test, then four weeks of leaving it alone. Have your module 1 map beside you.

1Map and write the task that breaks.
2Have someone new follow it once.
3Hand it over, with a decision and a limit.
4Check the four basics for everyone.
You’re off for two weeks. What breaks?The task only you know how to do. This is the one you work on.

Step 1a · map it before you write it

Trigger, handoffs, record

TriggerThe event that starts it. Not ‘monthly’.
HandoffsEvery point it changes hands: who → who.
RecordThe proof it happened, and where it lives.

Example, Kestrel invoicing: client signs off the event → account manager → founder → client → invoice in the client folder, tracker says Invoiced.

Step 1b · about 20 minutes

Write it: the nine questions

Steps last. If you can answer a question without thinking about your own business, you haven’t answered it yet. The full template and worked example are in the SOP guide.

1
What starts it?An event, not a timeframe.
2
Why does it exist?What it costs the business when it goes wrong.
3
What does it cover, and not?Point to who handles the edges.
4
Who owns it, does it, signs it off?One name for each.
5
What does done well look like?Numbers, not adjectives.
6
What do you need before you start?The actual system, folder or template.
7
Which steps would someone get wrong?Only those. Verb first. Say why.
8
What can they decide, and when do they stop?Decide alone up to… Stop and ask when…
9
How do we know it happened?The record, and the next review date.
