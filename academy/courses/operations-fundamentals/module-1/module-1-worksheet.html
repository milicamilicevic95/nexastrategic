Operations Fundamentals · Module 1

Your ball.

About 30 minutes. Do it for your own business. Nobody marks this; it’s yours, and you’ll use it in every module that follows.

1Map your eight parts. One name on each.
2Name your weakest part.
3Turn one live decision through all eight.

What sits in each part

Use these prompts to spot what belongs where. Cross out what doesn’t apply, add what’s missing.

PairPartPrompts
The workProcessHow an order or project goes start to finish · the tools you run on · where files live · passwords and access · anything written down
PeopleHiring paperwork · contracts · getting people started · holidays, sickness, leave · people leaving · who does what
The moneyCommercialPrices · quotes and proposals · discounts · payment terms · customer contracts · who can agree non-standard terms
FinanceInvoicing · chasing payment · paying suppliers · payroll · bookkeeping · setting tax aside · knowing your cash
The rulesLegalHow the company is set up · contracts with customers, suppliers, staff · who owns the work · renewals
ComplianceFilings and registrations · personal data · health and safety · insurance · licences · showing you follow the rules
ControlGovernanceWho signs contracts · who approves spending · who decides hires and prices · who gets told · how often you check
RiskWhat would hurt most · relying on one client, supplier or person · backups · what you’d do if it happened

This map is for recognition only. It isn’t a legal or compliance check. Which obligations apply depends on your business, your sector and where you operate. Module 4 covers how to find out, and when to call a professional.
