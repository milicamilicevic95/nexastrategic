# Masterclasses

## 1 · Know Enough (20 November 2026, 60 minutes)

*Bookkeeper, lawyer or ops hire: what does your business need now, and next?* Draft.

You don't need to be the operator. You need to know enough to know when. Founders learn what good enough looks like in each panel of operations, when a panel has outgrown them, who to bring in (smallest step first), and how to brief them precisely.
