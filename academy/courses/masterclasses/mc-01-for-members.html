Founders House · Masterclass 1 · 20 November

Know Enough.

Bookkeeper, lawyer or ops hire: what does your business need now, and next?

The idea

Founders don’t need to become the operations person. They need to know enough to know when to bring someone in, who, and what to ask.

Most founders think operations means process. Process is one part of it. I'll show them the whole picture, then give them two tests to run on their own business:

NOWThe house isn’t on fire.
I understand what’s happening, and the company can exist.
NEXTIf I stepped away, what breaks?
And where would we still need a person’s judgement?

Once a business has paying clients and comfortable runway, it needs a written plan, and every part of the business has to answer to it. That's my view, and two well-known Harvard Business Review models of how businesses grow back it: Churchill and Lewis, and Greiner.

What founders leave with

The Know Enough Map scored for their own business, one red area picked, and a four-part brief ready to send that week. It isn't advice: it shows them how to ask, not when they need a professional.
