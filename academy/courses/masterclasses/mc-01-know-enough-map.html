The brief.

2 · The decision I needWhat I’ll decide with your answer.
3 · Out of scopeWhat I’m not asking for.
4 · Fee cap, and by whenBudget or fixed fee, and the deadline.

This week

One red.

1. Score every row, Now and Next: red, amber or green.

2. Pick one red.

3. Write the brief for it, on the left.

4. Send it to the smallest right person on the ladder.

Not this: “Can you have a look at our contracts?”

This: “Funding is tighter and two teams overlap. We need the fair process for a restructure before we decide whether to start one. We don’t need a handbook or policies. Fixed fee, answer by Friday.”
