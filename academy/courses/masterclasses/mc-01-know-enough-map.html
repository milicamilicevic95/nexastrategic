This week

One red.

1. Score every row red, amber or green.

2. Pick one red.

3. Write the brief for it, on the left.

4. Send it to the smallest right person on the ladder.

Not this: “Can you have a look at our contracts?”

This: “Our biggest client contract renews next month. We need to know if clauses 8 and 12 let them exit early and what we’d owe. We don’t need a full review. Fixed fee, answer by Friday.”
