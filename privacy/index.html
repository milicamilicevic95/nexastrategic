Who we are

For anything about your personal information, email privacy@nexastrategic.com. Concentric is a separate activity with its own privacy notice. This notice does not cover it.

Whose information this covers

People who visit our website, contact us or enquire about our services, including through a Meta (Facebook or Instagram) lead form. People who subscribe to our newsletter. Contacts at our clients and suppliers. People we work with through a client, such as a client’s staff, or members of a programme we advise within. People connected with businesses we review for a client in operational due diligence, such as their owners, staff and customers.

What we collect and where it comes from

From you: your name, job title, company, email address, phone number, what you tell us in an enquiry, messages and meeting notes, and your marketing preferences. From Meta: when you complete one of our lead forms, Meta passes us the details you entered. From our clients: contact and working information about their staff, and records we need to provide our services. Automatically: limited technical information needed to run and secure the website.

We ask clients to give us employee and customer records in anonymised or summary form wherever that is enough. Where due diligence needs records that may contain special category data, such as absence or health information, we look at them only as far as the review needs, do not copy them where we can avoid it, and rely on the substantial public interest and legal claims conditions in the Data Protection Act 2018 as applicable.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your information, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time, and unsubscribe from any email. Email privacy@nexastrategic.com. We will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk, though we would like the chance to put it right first.

Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to deliver and secure the website. If we add analytics or marketing cookies, we will ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.

Security and changes

We use access controls, two-factor authentication where available, and providers with recognised security standards. No online system is completely secure. We will update this notice when our services or providers change, and tell you about material changes where we need to.
