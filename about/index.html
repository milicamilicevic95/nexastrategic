About

Made redundant at 24. Chief of Staff at 27.

I’m Mila Milićević. I’m 31, I live in London, and I build the part of a business that lets its founder step back.

They’d delegated the work. They hadn’t delegated the responsibility.

Three years before I became a Chief of Staff, I lost my job and had to start again. By 27 I was working alongside founders and senior leadership teams, helping them make decisions and run their businesses. I went on to become a COO in financial technology.

Different companies, different people, the same problem. Founders hired people to take work off their plates, then found themselves managing those people, answering their questions and making their decisions.

Often it wasn’t that they didn’t want to let go. Nobody had put the structure in place for anyone else to take responsibility. That’s the part I build.
